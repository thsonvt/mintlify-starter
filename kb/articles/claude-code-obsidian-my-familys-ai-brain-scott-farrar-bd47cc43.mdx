## Full Article

Scott Farrar is a math educator and parent who got tired of AI chats that drift and forget. So he built a household brain in Claude Code: a folder of Markdown files, viewed in Obsidian, that knows his appliances, warranties, insurance documents, and everything on his familys to-do list. In this episode, Scott shows how he uses it to shop for car insurance, plan repairs, share the system with his wife, and put priority tasks and recent wins on a wall display.
