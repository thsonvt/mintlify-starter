## Summary

The article explores various proverbs from the Go programming language, highlighting their wisdom and relevance in software development.

## Key Insights

> "A little copying is better than a little dependency."
>
> — Discussing the importance of code reuse and minimizing dependencies.

> "Don't communicate by sharing memory, share memory by communicating."
>
> — Emphasizing the value of communication in concurrent programming.
