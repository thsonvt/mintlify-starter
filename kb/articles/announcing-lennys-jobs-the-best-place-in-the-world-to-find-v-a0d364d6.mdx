Learn more.My goal is to help you succeed in your career.Ive spent thousands of hours on my newsletter and podcast sharing advice on how to get better at your work. But all that advice doesnt mean much if you dont actually have a jobor work youre excited about.Over the past six months, Ive been thinking long and hard about what more I can do to help people who are struggling to find a job they love.Today Im excited to announce the launch of Lennys Jobsthe best place in the world to find, vet, and land your dream job What makes Lennys Jobs unique and awesome:A laser focus on four builder roles at tech companies: product management, engineering, design, and growth/marketing. We focus exclusively on these roles because theyre what most of you are looking forand because these roles are starting to meld together. We aggregate open roles from top startups and big tech companies, and unlike any other job directory, we vet every company we include and filter out ghost roles and generic staffing-agency posts. This is the highest-quality directory of open tech roles youll find anywhere.Unlisted roles you wont find on any other job site. Every month I poll my audience on X and LinkedIn for roles theyre hiring for, many of which arent listed elsewhere. Along with submissions from our private reader Slack community, we integrate these roles into the directory. Look out for the Lennys Community tag in the feed.The Lenny 100. To help you prioritize your search, Ive curated a list of the top 100 companies Id most want to join if I were looking for a job. These are companies that IMHO have the highest talent density, are tackling the most ambitious problems, have the biggest upside potentialand are actively hiring. Ill update this list regularly.Custom-built AI tools embedded within each job post. For every role, you can quickly rate your fit, prep for the interview, develop an outreach plan, customize your resume, and more. Im really excited about the potential here.A custom-built AI interview and career coach. Incorporating Noam Segals incredible set of AI skills that readers have been raving about, the AI coach will help you with everything from building your pitch to doing mock interviews to analyzing and debriefing to handling compensation conversations and negotiating job offers. I dont believe theres anything out there like this, and we have some big ideas for where this coach can go. Try it out!An AI agent that emails you the best new jobs for you. Tell us what youre looking for and well automatically notify you of newly posted jobs that would be a great fit. Set it up once and youre all set. This is the only feature available exclusively for paid subscribers.Rich company data and tech-specific filters. Every job post includes up-to-date data on company funding, layoff history, headcount growth, compensation, and employee sentiment, so you can quickly evaluate opportunities without tab-hopping across six different sites. Plus, more than 100 tech-specific search filtersincluding investors, valuation, and tech stackhelp you find exactly what youre looking for.Curated interview and career resources. Ive organized a small but powerful collection of my favorite third-party content, tools, and courses that have moved the needle for many people I know on interview prep, speaking confidence, choosing where to work, and more. I also share my favorite podcast episodes and newsletter posts on career and interviewing advice.The site is free to use (and will continue to be), but paid Lennys Newsletter subscribers get higher AI feature limits, unlimited personalized results, and the proactive AI agent that notifies you about new jobs. To activate these benefits, first create a Lennys Jobs account, then link your paid subscription here.A huge shout-out to Amit Taylor, founder of TrueUp, who Ive partnered with on numerous State of the Product Job Market reports, for building this site with me. Also, a big thank-you to Ben Erez, Tal Raviv, and Noam Segal for tons of great feedback as this came together. Were excited to get your feedback and keep making it better and more useful to you. And weve got some big plans:Integrated white-glove recruiting services: direct intros between standout candidates and great companiesPersonalized career and skills development: coaching, training, and curated recommendations for the best stuff out thereOngoing state-of-the-job-market reports: data-backed updates on how the job market, roles, and skills are changing so that you can stay aheadI dont want to overpromise here, but I firmly believe theres no better place for product managers, engineers, designers, and growth/marketing professionals to discover high-quality open roles.Check out Lennys Jobs and let us know what you think.P.S.
