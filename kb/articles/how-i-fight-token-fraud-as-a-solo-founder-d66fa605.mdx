## Full Article

Today Im walking you through a problem that has cost me tens of thousands of dollars over the past year and eaten almost all of my engineering energy: token fraud. Im the solo founder of ChatPRD, a bootstrapped AI platform for product managers, and fraudsters have been farming our free trials, reselling promo codes, wrapping the product in relays, and installing a custom MCP that ran as a 24/7 compute worker. Ill walk through the six abuse patterns Ive seen, the business and technical controls I put in place, the Jev loop that classifies fraud in real time, and why I finally killed free trials for good.
