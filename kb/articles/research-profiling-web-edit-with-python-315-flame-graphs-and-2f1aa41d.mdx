## Full Article

Research Profiling web-edit with Python 3.15: flame graphs and source heatmaps — Profiling the unmodified web-edit app with Python 3.15’s built-in `profiling.sampling` revealed that mixed line-ending preservation dominated a busy editing workload: `preserve_endings` appeared in 64.8% of 15,296 recorded CPU stacks, with `SequenceMatcher.find_longest_match` contributing 38.3% of leaf samples. Sampling all threads was essential, since 95.1% of stacks came from AnyIO’s worker thread and would have been missed by a main-thread-only profile.
