## Summary

Eric Nguyen discusses the intersection of AI and bio-security, emphasizing the need for open models to defend against biological threats in an evolving arms race.

## Key Insights

> "AI security won't be solved by one company in secret. Open source puts these tools in every defender's hands."
>
> — Quoted from Adrien Carreira's tweet referenced in the article.

> "Defense is currently losing this battle, but Radical Numerics argues to push the frontier harder!"
>
> — Eric Nguyen's perspective on the current state of bio-security.

> "Can the model continue that trajectory on its own?"
>
> — Discussion on the capabilities of models thinking in DNA.
