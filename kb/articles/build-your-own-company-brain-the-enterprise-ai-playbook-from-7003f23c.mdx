Sharadh Krishnamurthy is an engineering manager at Stripe, where he helped build Kai, the companys internal AI agent used by more than 10,000 employees every week. Hes worked across several of Stripes core infrastructure teams, including data and developer experience, which gives him a grounded, systems-level perspective on what it actually takes to make AI work at enterprise scale.
