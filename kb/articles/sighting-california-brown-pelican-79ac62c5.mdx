## Summary

The California Brown Pelican has taken over the Pacifica Pier in San Mateo County, CA, after its closure due to safety concerns.

## Key Insights

> "The Pacifica Pier shut down at the start of June after a crack in the concrete walkway made access to the pier unsafe."
>
> — Describing the reason for the pier's closure.
