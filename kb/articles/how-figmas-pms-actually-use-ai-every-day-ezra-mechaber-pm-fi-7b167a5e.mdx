## Full Article

Ezra Mechaber is a growth product manager at Figma whose goal is to be lazy in the right places. When he joined Figma, he found almost no one outside engineering using AI to get real work done, so he and a couple of fellow PMs turned his personal Claude Code setup into PM OS, a shared repo of 31 skills that every PM at Figma now uses.
