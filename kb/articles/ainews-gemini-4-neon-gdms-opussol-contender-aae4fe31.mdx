## Full Article

Google DeepMind shipped Gemini 4 Neon, its fourth Gemini 4 model in about seven weeks. If you’re confused about the naming, you’re not alone; the 1.5 month old Argon is now retired, the old Pro and Flash from Gemini 1-3 are dead, and if you’re thinking the names are based on atomic weights, Neon (10), the midtier model is faster/cheaper than Carbon (6). This is a helpful guide to the current leading models and sizes: Because of how distillation, RL, and benchmarking works, Neon technically outperforms Carbon by some measures despite being a smaller model… Also today, Anthropic priced its IPO at $83 per share, above its initial range, and Palo Alto Networks agreed to buy Applied Compute in a $4.5B all-stock deal. Anthropic · Palo Alto NetworksAI News for November 17–18, 2026. We checked 12 subreddits and 544 Twitter accounts. Browse past issues.AI Twitter RecapGemini 4 NeonLaunch and availabilityPricing and specs. Neon costs $3 for input, $0.15 for cached input and $15 for output per million tokens. It has a 1M-token context window and four effort levels. Vals reports a maximum output limit of 1M tokens. Google · ValsRollout. Neon is coming to the paid Gemini API in AI Studio and Android Studio, Gemini for business, and Antigravity for Pro and Ultra subscribers. In the Gemini app, Pro and Ultra users can select it as “Deeper analysis,” including for Tasks. Google’s rollout · Gemini appDay-one integrations. Cursor, GitHub Copilot across its app, CLI and VS Code, and LangChain Managed Deep Agents. Release cadence. Arena counted gaps of 28 days between Argon and Carbon, 15 between Carbon and Hydrogen, and six between Hydrogen and Neon. ArenaIndependent resultsHigh versus xhigh. At high effort, Neon matched Carbon’s 60 on the Intelligence Index for $3.74 per task. On GDP.pdf it scored 33.4% at high and 28.0% at xhigh. Artificial AnalysisAgent Arena. Neon debuted second with 1,336 points across about 4,200 sessions. It had a $2.82 median task cost and placed second on steerability. Carbon still leads the separate Text Arena. Agent Arena · Text ArenaVals. Neon placed second on the Vals Index at 70.9%, about 0.4 points behind Fable 5.2 at less than half the cost per task. On Code Migration it averaged 160 turns, fewer than Carbon’s 234 or Hydrogen’s 574, and scored below both. Vals Index · Code MigrationCursorBench 4.1. Neon scored 63.7% at default effort, up from Hydrogen’s 57.3%. CursorHands-on reportsSteering and capacity. In 13 early Antigravity sessions, Kimmonismus recorded 762 tool calls and a 93% input-cache hit rate. When told to avoid the terminal, Neon ran zero shell commands in those tests. The logs also recorded 57 capacity errors and four stalled runs. Test logsHow teams use it. Theo says he’s now using Neon for copy, Fable to implement changes, and Astra to playtest. Jeremy Howard praised Neon while saying he was reverting Solveit’s Astra and Sol options to their pre-6.2 versions. Theo · Jeremy HowardNew results for GPT-6.2Math and formal proof. Astra and Sol took the top two spots on MathArena. Astra solved 42 of 53 ArXivLean problems. Vals separately reports what it believes is the first formalization in Lean of Smoktunowicz’s Gap Theorem, with the proof checked by an independent kernel replay. Tim Gehr · ValsAgent Arena. Astra debuted sixth. Sol placed eleventh at a $0.90 median task cost. On the separate Code Arena WebDev board, Astra placed third at a 42% lower median cost than its predecessor. Agent Arena · WebDevBackground discounts. OpenAI opened a public beta for GPT-6.2 Responses API jobs that can wait. Two-hour and eight-hour targets for the first response come with 30% and 40% discounts per token, respectively. OpenAI DevelopersWhat changed after launch. OpenAI’s Tibo Sottiaux says early reviewers used builds from before the final few days of changes. Artificial Analysis found Astra’s responses got shorter and it was less likely to admit uncertainty in its behavioral evaluation. Sottiaux · Artificial AnalysisOpen weights and inferenceCorrection to yesterday’s Pi adapter numbers. Yesterday’s issue cited 15 to 32 solves on the 51 hardest SWE-bench Pro tasks. Hugging Face reran affected tasks after finding a tunnel outage that hit nine base-model runs. The corrected result is 20 to 33 solves, or 39.2% to 64.7%. It also released an OpenCode adapter that moved Qwen3.8-27B from 35.3% to 51.0% on the same subset with thinking off. Corrected model card · OpenCode releaseTencent releases Hy4 Flash weights. The 224B-parameter multimodal MoE activates 12B parameters per token, supports 1M context and includes a roughly 64B n-gram embedding table. Tencent released instruct, FP8 and base checkpoints. Tencent repository · vLLMvLLM 0.34.0. The extensible KV cache is on by default and Model Runner V2 is the sole GPU runner. The team reports up to 1.95× faster NVFP4 decoding on GB200. vLLMInference capacity grew faster than peak compute. Epoch estimates that the capacity of chips sold for a fixed basket of agent workloads grew 4.6× from Q2 2025 to Q2 2026, while peak 8-bit compute grew 2.6×. Epoch AIRunway cuts serving hardware in half. It reports serving a 57B video model at 24 fps on four GPUs, down from eight. RunwayAgent tooling. Prime Intellect launched Model Factory for RL, supervised fine-tuning, serving and evals. Hugging Face released the message dataset from its 1,000-agent PyBot project. Prime Intellect · Hugging FaceResearch and chip designOpenAI models help redesign AMD circuits. OpenAI reports up to 14% improvement against optimized baselines on blocks of AMD’s flagship GPU design. The team used Synopsys tools to check that rewritten circuits preserved behavior and to measure power, performance and area. Some changes made it into a design sent for manufacturing. OpenAIMeasuring research acceleration. OpenAI published an update on internal agent use and research task completion, and proposed regular disclosure of indicators for AI research acceleration. OpenAISmaller harness edits can save tokens. In a 40-issue held-out test, a gated approach that edited one module at a time scored 40.0% on SWE-bench Lite at 46.7K tokens per task. A full rewrite scored 42.5% at 451.6K tokens. DAIR research summaryAgent teamwork. MAC Bench finds most gains from eight agents came from exploring in parallel. Opus 5.5 was the sole tested model to show an added gain from coordination. Bespoke LabsMedia and voiceGenie 4 reaches Project Genie. Google’s world model adds native audio and lets users change the world with text mid-session. Access is rolling out to eligible Google AI Ultra subscribers. Google DeepMindTurn detection and transcription. Inworld released weights for a voice-isolation model and its SAVA turn detector, which it says detects completed turns in a median 71 ms. AssemblyAI split its 3.7 release into models for voice agents, live transcription and recordings. Inworld · AssemblyAIKling Image 4.0. It placed seventh in Artificial Analysis’s text-to-image rankings and sixth in editing. Artificial AnalysisIndustryAnthropic prices its IPO at $83. The offering covers 980M shares, about $81.3B at the offer price. About $79.4B of that comes from shares sold by Anthropic before fees. Nasdaq trading under ANT is expected November 19. AnthropicPalo Alto Networks agrees to buy Applied Compute. The $4.5B all-stock deal gives Palo Alto a platform for post-training and serving open-weight models. The companies expect to close in Palo Alto’s fiscal Q3 2027, subject to regulatory clearance and other conditions. Palo Alto NetworksAI Reddit RecapLocal audioBlue TTS v3.
