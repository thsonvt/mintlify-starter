## Full Article

GPT‑6.2 Astra and GPT‑6.2 Sol. OpenAI: We’re introducing GPT‑6.2 Astra, our new frontier model for your hardest projects, and GPT‑6.2 Sol, our efficient, versatile model for everyday work. They're available in the API as gpt-6.2-astra and gpt-6.2-sol. I shipped LLM 0.38 with support for the new models. 6.2 Astra is 80% of the price of 6 Astra - $8/MTok input and $40/MTok output, the same price as Claude Fable 5.2 (which was also 80% of its predecessor). 6 Astra and Fable 5 were both $10/$50. The cached price is an even bigger discount - $0.40/million compared to $1/million for the older model. This is great news for longer running agentic sessions. GPT-6.2 Sol is the same $2/$10 price as 6.1 Sol and 6 Sol - which are half the price of GPT-5.6 Sol ($4/$20). Here are the pelicans for GPT-6.2 Sol and for GPT-6.2 Astra. The buzz around these new models is mixed at the moment. Astra 6.2 isn't scoring quite as high as Fable 5.2 on various benchmarks, and I've seen a few complaints that its communication style can be confusing. Theo Browne's review (in the form of a feedback letter he sent OpenAI during pre-release) has this useful example: When this model is working on long tasks, it can pull in a lot of subagents and manage them pretty well.
