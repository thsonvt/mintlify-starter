## Full Article

Daniel Blum is a product manager at Melio, a B2B payments company, and one of the most systematic thinkers Ive had on the show when it comes to personal AI infrastructure. Hes spent the past year building a Claude- and Cowork-based productivity system that manages his Notion board, processes his Slack and email, and runs self-improvement loops every week without needing to be prompted.
